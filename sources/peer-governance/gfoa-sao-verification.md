# GFOA Best Practices and SAO Citation: Source Verification

Verified April 5, 2026.

## GFOA "Long-Term Financial Planning" Best Practice (2022)

- **Source:** https://www.gfoa.org/materials/long-term-financial-planning
- **Board approval date:** March 4, 2022
- **Key language:** "GFOA recommends that all governments prepare and maintain a long-term financial plan that projects revenues, expenses, financial position, and external factors for all key funds and government operations at least five years into the future."
- **Scope:** "All governments" — no exclusions stated. When GFOA intends a narrower scope, its other best practices use "general-purpose governments" (see Fund Balance guideline below). The use of "all governments" here is deliberately inclusive.

## GFOA "Budget Monitoring" Best Practice (2018)

- **Source:** https://www.gfoa.org/materials/budget-monitoring
- **Board approval date:** March 31, 2018
- **Key language on comparison basis:** Recommends budget-to-actual reporting include "previous year actuals," "averages of several prior year actuals," and "projections and forecasts" as bases of comparison.
- **Key language on impact:** Under "Time frame" analysis: "How does the current spending pattern impact the subsequent year's budget?"
- **Scope:** "All governments"

## GFOA scope: "All governments" vs. "general-purpose governments"

- **Fund Balance guideline** (https://www.gfoa.org/materials/fund-balance-guidelines-for-the-general-fund): Uses "general-purpose governments" — a deliberately narrower scope. Also references school districts as examples of entities within GFOA's purview, confirming special-purpose districts are part of the GFOA universe.
- **Long-Term Financial Planning** and **Budget Monitoring** best practices both use "all governments" — the broadest language available.
- **GFOA membership explicitly includes special districts:** The GFOA membership page (gfoa.org/join) lists "Special District" as a named membership category, defined as "Independent public entities such as utilities, transit agencies, fire protection, retirement systems, or water authorities." Library districts are independent public entities in this category. Other organizational membership categories: City, County, School District, Native American/Indigenous Governments.
- **GFOA membership scale:** "More than 30,000 members are federal, state/provincial, and local finance officials" (gfoa.org/about-gfoa).
- **TRL's own finance staff treated GFOA as applicable:** Finance Director Lowell, June 2020 board meeting: "governmental entities submit a report to GFOA" — in TRL context.
- **Library districts are local governments under Washington law:** Established as municipal corporations under RCW 27.12. Audited by SAO under RCW 43.09, same authority used for cities and counties.

## Washington SAO cites GFOA best practices

### Strongest evidence:

1. **"Top 12 most important financial policies"** (SAO blog, June 18, 2021)
   - URL: https://sao.wa.gov/the-audit-connection-blog/2021/top-12-most-important-financial-policies
   - Opens with: "According to the Government Finance Officers Association (GFOA), it's a best practice for governments to formally adopt financial policies."
   - Links to six specific GFOA best-practice documents as recommended resources for local governments, including:
     - GFOA Long-term planning and forecasting policy template
     - GFOA Investment policy best practices
     - GFOA Example policies for reserves
     - GFOA Revenue control policy best practices
     - GFOA Capital asset management best practices
     - GFOA Debt management policy best practices

2. **BARS Manual — GFOA Financial Reporting Recognition Programs** (dedicated section)
   - URL: https://sao.wa.gov/bars-annual-filing/bars-gaap-manual/reporting/gfoa-financial-reporting-recognition-programs
   - States: "The Government Finance Officers Association (GFOA) provides guidance on what to include in the ACFR..."
   - GFOA has its own section within the official BARS manual that SAO requires local governments to follow.

3. **"What's the scenario? Financial planning in uncertain times"** (SAO blog, 2020)
   - URL: https://sao.wa.gov/the-audit-connection-blog/2020/whats-scenario-financial-planning-uncertain-times
   - Directs local governments to GFOA's free webinar on financial scenario planning and to gfoa.org/fiscal-first-aid

4. **"Options are available for moving to 'no contact' cash receipting"** (SAO blog, 2020)
   - URL: https://sao.wa.gov/the-audit-connection-blog/2020/options-are-available-moving-no-contact-cash-receipting
   - States: "The Government Finance Officers Association (GFOA) recommends that governments evaluate lockbox services"
   - Cites: "GFOA -- Best Practices: Use of Lockbox Services"

### Assessment:
The SAO cites GFOA best practices across multiple categories of advisory publications: the official BARS manual, the Audit Connection blog, and standalone best-practice PDFs. The "Top 12" post explicitly frames GFOA best practices as the standard local governments should follow.
