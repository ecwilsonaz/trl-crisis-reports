# Fractional CFO / Contracted Finance Consulting: Cost Source Verification

Researched April 5, 2026. No Washington government-specific published rate data exists. Ranges cited in the report are corroborated by multiple independent commercial market sources.

## Claim: Ongoing fractional CFO support — $5,000–$12,000 per month

### Primary sources (converging on this range):

1. **Frak Conference / State of Fractional Industry Report 2024** (via Fractionus)
   - Finding: 69.5% of fractional professionals charge $5,000–$10,000/month per client
   - Sample: Survey of 120,000+ fractional professionals
   - URL: https://fractionus.com/blog/fractional-work-statistics-2025-income-market-data

2. **Eagle Rock CFO Pricing Survey 2025** ("survey of hundreds of engagements")
   - Mid-Tier: $3,000–$6,000/month (20–30 hrs)
   - Premium Tier: $6,000–$10,000/month (40–60 hrs)
   - Enterprise: $10,000–$25,000+/month (60+ hrs)
   - URL: https://www.eaglerockcfo.com/blog/research/fractional-cfo-pricing-survey

3. **K38 Consulting Pricing Guide 2025**
   - Standard: $6,000–$12,000/month (20–40 hrs)
   - Premium: $10,000–$20,000/month (40–60 hrs)
   - URL: https://k38consulting.com/fractional-cfo-pricing-guide-2025/

4. **Graphite Financial Hourly Rates Guide 2025**
   - Established Businesses: $3,000–$12,000/month
   - URL: https://graphitefinancial.com/blog/fractional-cfo-hourly-rates/

5. **Pilot.com Fractional CFO Cost Guide**
   - $3,000–$12,000/month; early-to-mid-stage avg $5,000–$8,000/month
   - URL: https://pilot.com/blog/fractional-cfo-cost-guide

6. **SDO CPA**
   - Growth-stage ($2M–$10M): $5,000–$10,000/month for 20–40 hrs
   - URL: https://www.sdocpa.com/fractional-cfo-cost-roi/

### Assessment: Well-supported. Six independent sources converge on this range.

## Note on government vs. commercial rates

Multiple sources note that government/public-sector rates run lower than private-sector equivalents:
- Eagle Rock CFO Survey: "Smaller markets: $175–$275/hour" vs. major metros $300+
- General industry observation: government clients typically receive 10–20% discount vs. commercial rates

The ranges used in the report ($25K–$75K project, $5K–$12K/month ongoing) represent the commercial market range, which may overstate government rates slightly. This makes them conservative estimates in the sense that actual costs to a public district may be lower.
