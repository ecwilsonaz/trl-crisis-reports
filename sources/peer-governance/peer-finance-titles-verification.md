# Peer Library System Finance Leadership: Title Verification

Verified April 5, 2026. All titles confirmed from primary sources (official websites, published board documents).

## Peer Systems

### Sno-Isle Libraries
- **Title:** Director of Finance
- **Holder:** Janella Lewis
- **Source:** Official leadership page, sno-isle.org/leadership/
- **Archived:** April 5, 2026
- **Notes:** Listed under "Department Directors" alongside Director of HR, Director of Spaces & Technology, etc. Reports to Executive Director (Eric Howard).

### Pierce County Library System
- **Title:** Finance Director
- **Holder:** Mary Stimson
- **Source:** Official contact page, mypcls.org/contact-us/
- **Archived:** April 5, 2026
- **Notes:** Listed alongside other directors (Facilities & Capital Projects Director, IT Director, etc.). PCLS also has a separate "Business & Compliance Director" (Clifford Jo), who previously held a combined "Director of Finance and Business Operations" role and won the 2015 Government Financial Executive of the Year award.

### Fort Vancouver Regional Library District
- **Title:** Finance Director
- **Holder:** Catrina Galicz
- **Source:** Official administrative team page, fvrl.org/about/administrative-team/
- **Archived:** April 5, 2026
- **Notes:** "Supervises the accounting, finance, and procurement/purchasing staff and functions." Seventeen years of governmental audit and accounting experience.

### Whatcom County Library System
- **Title:** Director of Finance and Administration
- **Holder:** Jackie Saul
- **Source:** WCLS Board of Trustees Regular Meeting Minutes, January 21, 2025 (in board packet: wcls-board-packet-2025-02.pdf, page 5). Staff listed "In Attendance" includes "Jackie Saul, Director of Finance and Administration." Also listed as preparer of consent agenda cover sheet (page 3).
- **Archived:** Board packet already in sources/peer-governance/wcls-board-packet-2025-02.pdf
- **Notes:** WCLS is the smallest system in the comparison (~160K population served). Despite smaller size, uses "Director" title for finance lead.

### Kitsap Regional Library
- **Title:** Deputy Director, Chief Financial & Operating Officer
- **Holder:** Dan Baer
- **Source:** Official staff directory, krl.org/our-people/
- **Archived:** April 5, 2026
- **Notes:** Dual-hat role combining finance with overall operations at the second-in-command level. Highest finance title among all systems in the comparison. Kitsap serves ~275K population — roughly half of TRL's service area.

### King County Library System (KCLS)
- **Title:** Director of Finance and Facilities Management
- **Holder:** Donna Zirkle
- **Source:** Official administration page, kcls.org/administration/
- **Archived:** April 5, 2026
- **Notes:** "Oversees financial planning and compliance, as well as capital asset operations and maintenance." Holds a Certified Managerial Accountant (CMA) credential. Combined finance + facilities role.
