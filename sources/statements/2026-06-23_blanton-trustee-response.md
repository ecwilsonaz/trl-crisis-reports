# Hal Blanton (TRL Trustee, Lewis County) — response to request for comment on the "desired outcomes" document

**Respondent:** Hal Blanton, one of Lewis County's two TRL trustees
**Received:** June 23, 2026, 1:35 p.m. Pacific (email reply to Eric Wilson's June 17 request for comment)
**From:** hblanton@trl.org (official TRL address; carries the standard Public Records Act / chapter 42.56 RCW disclosure footer)
**Status:** On the record. Press comment given for publication in response to a request for comment; written answers.

## Format

Eric sent five numbered questions on June 17. Blanton answered inline. His answers are reproduced verbatim below, each under the question it responds to. He answered all five, though Q2 and Q5 are answered at a general level rather than to the specific point asked.

---

### Q1 — Did Mittge consult you/the board first? Proper process? Has it been adopted/voted on?
> Brian put together some conversation points to be discussed by the board. It's not an official document, nor has it been discussed at a board meeting. It is a common practice that has been followed in the past to start conversations about policy items.

### Q2 — Then ("no critique," June 2022) vs. now ("ending rainbow and activist displays"): consistent, a change, or a different question?
> It is important that the public be aware of how decisions are made, what the policies and procedures are that are used in the operation of TRL.
>
> The process of review and criticism is an important aspect of setting policy and maintaining an agreed-on ethos.

### Q3 — "How do we protect children"; does moving materials "about sex, sexual and gender orientation" include a picture book featuring a same-sex couple/LGBTQ family, or only materials that teach about sex?
> Society has a shared responsibility to protect children from harm, both physically and mentally. We(society) regulate in the workplace with child-labor laws; We have rules for age of consent regarding tattoos and piercings; We have seatbelt restraint mandates geared toward age ; We have movie guidelines ; Advertising is regulated with age as a factor; Recently there are court cases dealing with social media platforms availability by age….There can be guardrails installed to ensure that material can be sorted in a manner that can shield children from content that their parents wouldn't want them exposed to without parental involvement.

(Note: does NOT answer the specific picture-book vs. teaches-about-sex distinction asked; answers the general child-protection rationale.)

### Q4 — Do shelving/display decisions belong with the professional librarians, the board, or the president acting on his own?
> I think it can be a shared dialogue as part of the process. Public should have an opportunity to help guide decisions.

### Q5 — Levy Lid Lift Committee: does a document like this help or hurt the district-wide majority, and what does the library need to earn it?
> I believe we best serve the people of our five county district by clearly showing them the operational process they are paying for, and they will make an informed decision about an increase in funding.

(Note: does NOT engage the help/hurt-the-levy question directly.)
