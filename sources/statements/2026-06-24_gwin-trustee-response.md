## Verbatim answers (June 22)

- **Q1 — Did Mittge consult you/the board first; has the board adopted/voted on it?**
  > No

- **Q2 — Proper process for a proposal like this to come before the board?**
  > Items need to be put on the Boards agenda to come before the Board. Any trustee or the Executive Director may propose items for the agenda.

- **Q3 — How do you see this document, and the response to it, in relation to the April "assessment"?**
  > I do not have a crystal ball the predicts the future. Our hope with this document is it will be a road map for the regular Executive Director and a cautionary historical document for future trustees.

- **Q4 — Do shelving/display decisions belong with the professional librarians, the board, or the president acting alone?**
  > Currently, the decision is with the librarians and library staff. Librarians and library staff possess the knowledge and expertise needed to make informed decisions about book classification organization and shelving.

- **Q5 — Do you support or oppose the document's proposals?**
  > I do not support Brian's proposal.
