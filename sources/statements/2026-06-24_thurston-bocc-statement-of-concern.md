## Full text (verbatim)

> **Statement of Concern regarding the Timberland Regional Library Board of Trustees**
>
> On June 12, 2026, Timberland Regional Library (TRL) Board of Trustees President Brian Mittge presented library leadership with a document outlining a list of "desired outcomes" for the library system. That document calls for the removal of LGBTQ+ materials from children's and youth sections, the elimination of Pride and similar displays, and the effective exclusion of unhoused patrons from library spaces. It directs library staff – in Mr. Mittge's own words – to "be beige, not rainbow."
>
> The Thurston County Board of County Commissioners condemns this document in the clearest possible terms.
>
> The TRL Board of Trustees has formally adopted the American Library Association's (ALA) Library Bill of Rights as policy. That document is unambiguous: materials shall not be restricted based on doctrinal disapproval, and a person's right to use a library shall not be denied or abridged because of origin, age, background, or views. Mr. Mittge's "desired outcomes" document violates TRL's own adopted policy and the foundational principles of public library service.
>
> We are also deeply concerned about the context in which this document was released. TRL is navigating a serious financial crisis that will require the trust and support of communities across all five counties to resolve. Actions that alienate library patrons, demoralize already-strained staff, and signal that some community members are less welcome than others undermine that trust at exactly the moment it is most needed.
>
> We believe the ALA Library Bill of Rights should be reaffirmed as binding policy and affirm that the Timberland Regional Library system exists to serve all members of our communities – without exception.
>
