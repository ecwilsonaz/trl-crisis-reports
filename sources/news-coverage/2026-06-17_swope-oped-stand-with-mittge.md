# Sean Swope op-ed: "I stand firmly with Brian Mittge; It's time to get libraries back to being libraries"

**Author:** Sean Swope (Republican Lewis County Commissioner, District 1 — Centralia + surrounding).
**Posted:** Wednesday, June 17, 2026, 3:54 pm. **Outlet:** The Chronicle (chronline) — Commentary. **URL:** https://www.chronline.com/stories/sean-swope-i-stand-firmly-with-brian-mittge-its-time-to-get-libraries-back-to-being-libraries,404286
**Note:** Published ~7 minutes after his 3:47 pm email reply to Eric's right-of-reply request (`.sources/comments-received/2026-06-17_swope-reply.md`). The op-ed is the fuller, public version of that statement — Swope is now on the record publicly, in his own byline, escalating.

## Verbatim (key passages)

> "As a Lewis County commissioner who helped appoint Brian Mittge to the Timberland Regional Library Board of Trustees, I am appalled by the vicious character assassination and scapegoating aimed at him."

> "Our libraries should be safe, neutral places where families go to check out books, study and learn. Period. Not hubs for activism. Not places to push sexual beliefs on kids. And not spots to divide children from their parents. Brian nailed it when he said libraries need to be 'beige, not rainbow.'"

> "I do not support banning books. Adults deserve access to a full range of materials, and every book has its proper place. But let's be honest: sexually explicit content, **books on gender and sexual orientation**, and pornographic illustrated material do not belong in the children's section. **Move them to the adult areas where they belong.**"

> "Defending what amounts to grooming — that encourages secrecy between kids and parents so children can access inappropriate and pornographic material behind their backs — is unacceptable." [re Centralia City Councilor Ray Chapman-Wilson]

> "That means safe spaces for families, no activism, **no therapists-in-training behind the desk**, and a real focus on providing ample books for public use."

> "**If the board and library leadership refuse to change course** and keep alienating families with this politicized approach, **I will fight hard against any levy lid lift. I will also look at every option to stop forcing Lewis County taxpayers to fund a system that no longer serves its original purpose.**"

> "Our libraries were built for books in rural communities — not social experiments."
