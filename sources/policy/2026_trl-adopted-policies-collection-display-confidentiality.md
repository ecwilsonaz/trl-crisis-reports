# TRL policies and guidelines — collection, displays, confidentiality (primary source)

Verbatim excerpts pulled 2026-06-17 from trl.org/policies-guidelines/ (verify against the live pages). Index: https://trl.org/policies-guidelines/ · All policies: https://trl.org/allpolicies/

Status of the documents: the numbered Policies below (No. 004, 006, 028, 075) are board-adopted; the Collection Guidelines are set by the professional staff in Collection Services (MLIS librarians and the department head) and are not board-adopted, per interim director Andrea Heisel (2026-06-24).

---

## 1. Collection Guidelines
URL: https://trl.org/policies-guidelines/collection-guidelines/ · Effective 2/17/2026 (the no-sequestering clause appears in archived snapshots back to Dec 7, 2023, under a self-stated effective date of 8/01/2022).

> "There shall be no prejudicial labeling, sequestering, or alteration of materials because of controversy surrounding the author or the subject matter."

> "None will be sequestered except for the purpose of protection against theft or injury."

> "The Board of Trustees delegates its authority to purchase library materials to Timberland Regional Library's Executive Director and their designees."

> "Library materials and resources will be selected and retained in a variety of formats and comprehension levels."

> "The Board of Trustees recognizes the right of individuals to question resources in the library collection. The library will give serious consideration to each patron's opinion. Material under consideration will remain available to patrons until a decision is made. Any patron questioning resources in the library collection may ask the library staff about such materials. Staff members will make every effort to reach resolution of the patron's concerns through discussion, before offering the form. Please refer to the Library Resource Review Procedure."

> "...Timberland Regional Library Board of Trustees has adopted the following documents from the American Library Association: ALA Freedom to Read, ALA Freedom to View, ALA Library Bill of Rights."

Selection criteria (verbatim list): mission support; community demand/interest; accuracy/timeliness; "Representation of ideas that are unique, alternative, experimental, or controversial"; "Reflects a diversity of human experience"; AI-authored items not selected.

## 2. Collections of Materials Policy
URL: https://trl.org/policies-guidelines/collections-of-materials-policy/ · Policy No. 004, effective 11/16/2022, review 2/1/2030.

> "The Executive Director is responsible for the selection of the materials. The Executive Director delegates this function to Collection Services Director and the Collection Development Librarians who follow TRL Collections Guidelines."

> "TRL supports intellectual freedom by providing collections that represent a diversity of cultures, views, and opinions."

> "TRL follows a formal review procedure to respond to concerns about items in the collection."

## 4. Public Display Spaces Policy
URL: https://trl.org/policies-guidelines/public-display-spaces-policy/ · Policy No. 028, effective 10/23/2024.

> displays operate "under guidelines and procedures established by the Executive Director"; "A request for the use of these spaces must be submitted to the Library Manager for approval."

> "Display space is made available to such persons or groups on equal terms, regardless of the beliefs or affiliations of group members."

> "The provision of display space for public use does not constitute TRL endorsement of the beliefs or viewpoints..."

> "Patrons concerned about current or future displays may use the form and procedures in place for Patron Opinion of Library Materials."

## 5. Reconsideration procedure
Named in the policies as the "Library Resource Review Procedure" (Collection Guidelines) and the "Collection Review Procedure" (Collections of Materials Policy); displays route to "Patron Opinion of Library Materials." Not located as a standalone published page on trl.org as of 2026-06-17.

## 6. Confidentiality of Library Records and Data Privacy Policy
