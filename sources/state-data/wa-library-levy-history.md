# Washington State Library Levy Lid Lift History

Compiled from Washington Secretary of State certified election results, EveryLibrary campaign history, and individual library district records. This list covers levy lid lifts (regular property tax levy increases requiring voter approval under RCW 84.55.050). It excludes excess levies, bonds, annexations, and district formation votes.

**This list is incomplete for pre-2005 elections.** The WA State Library elections database (sos.wa.gov/library/elections.aspx) is self-described as incomplete, and no single public source compiles all results. Pre-2005 levies are not included here because they could not be independently verified.

## Summary

- **Since 2005:** 28 passed, 6 failed — **82% success rate** (34 documented)
- **Since 2017:** 13 passed, 2 failed — **87% success rate** (15 documented)
- **Failures since 2005:** Kitsap 2007, TRL 2009, Kitsap 2010, Whatcom 2016, NOLS Prop 2 2018, Asotin 2025
- **Note:** Spokane 2015 ($22M capital bond, failed at 57.6% with 60% supermajority required) excluded per scope — bonds are a different legal mechanism.

## Known gaps

This list does not include:
- Pre-2005 levies (insufficient public records to verify)
- City library levies (e.g., Seattle Public Library, Spokane Public Library)
- Excess levies or bonds (different legal mechanism)
- Any levy attempts by systems not in the wa-library-districts.md research dataset

The WA State Library elections database (sos.wa.gov/library/elections.aspx) and EveryLibrary campaign history (everylibrary.org/campaign_history) are the two most comprehensive sources but are self-described as incomplete.
