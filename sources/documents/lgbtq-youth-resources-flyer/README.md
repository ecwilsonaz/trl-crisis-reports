# Timberland Regional Library — "Resources for LGBTQIA+ Kids & Families" flyer (June 2023)

TRL's "Resources for LGBTQIA+ Kids & Families" flyer, dated June 2023, referenced in the "desired outcomes" reporting.

## Published in the article

- `flyer-books-side.jpg` — the flyer's interior: a book-finding guide for young readers (juvenile "J" call numbers; recommended picture books, chapter books, graphic novels, and nonfiction), ending with the library-account **privacy line** ("No matter your age, you have the right to keep your library account private…"), the sentence Trustee Brian Mittge's "desired outcomes" document points to. This is the full-resolution archival copy; the downscaled web version is published at `images/flyer-books-side.jpg`.

## Held, not in this commit

The flyer's other side — crisis and support services for LGBTQ youth (Crisis Clinic, Trevor Project, Stonewall Youth, Planned Parenthood, Oasis Queer Youth Center), the cover panel (TRL logo, "June 2023"), and a "use any name" library-card box — is retained in working files only, pending a possible later use. It is not part of this commit.

## Provenance and handling

Obtained by this reporter from a source. The flyer was photographed; the image was **perspective-corrected and cropped** for legibility. **No content was altered.** A **red box was added by this reporter** to mark the privacy line at issue.

## Authenticity

Corroborated independently of the source:

1. The same flyer is **photographed at the top of Trustee Brian Mittge's "desired outcomes" document** (the privacy line is visible there), tying the artifact directly to the document the article reports on.
2. Interim director **Andrea Heisel confirmed on the record** the privacy line's exact wording and that the library revised it in spring 2026 (see `sources/statements/2026-06-23_heisel-pamphlet-clarification.md`).
